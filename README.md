MavTutor is a system that helps tutors and students schedule sessions that foster learning and academic growth. Tutors can list the courses for which they can help, while students list courses of interest to them. Sessions then group a tutor with one or more students into a scheduled time of practice, learning, and improvement. 

To ensure quality learning, participants in MavTutor can rate and write reviews for tutors, students, and individual tutoring sessions. In addition, comments can be made on the reviews for clarification and for discussing potential improvements.
